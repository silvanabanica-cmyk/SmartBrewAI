## Assumptions and uncertainties

1. Priorities: The rankings are an initial AI proposal. They have not been validated against stakeholder priorities or implementation dependencies.
2. Effort estimates: Story points are relative estimates and would need refinement by the development team.
3. Optional assistant: The local LLM assistant is optional and should not be treated as a mandatory component of every deployment.
4. Dependencies: Payment, recommendations, receipts, inventory, and fallback operation depend on other system components or services.
5. Scope: The backlog separates some behaviors—such as ingredient availability and transaction recording—that could alternatively be grouped into broader backlog items.
6. Unspecified details: Offline payments, inventory thresholds, recommendation logic, data retention, and mood-analysis consent require stakeholder clarification.
